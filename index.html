What it does

Your day, sorted. Out loud.

Noted listens, understands what you meant, and keeps up when you change your mind.

Plan my day

Ask Noted to plan your day. It works around your meetings, puts overdue and urgent things first, groups quick errands and leaves room for lunch. Say yes and it sets the times.

Habits that stick

Tap a ring to log it: 8 glasses of water, 20 pages, the gym on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Streaks only count the days you planned, so a rest day never breaks them.

Lists for everything

Groceries, packing, movies to watch. Say "add milk and eggs to groceries" or "I got the milk", and Noted keeps the list.

Roast mode 🔥

Turn it on and Noted roasts you, playfully, when tasks slip. Never mean, and it backs off when you're stressed.

"1 task overdue. Procrastination isn't a personality."

Arabic, English, or both

Speak Egyptian, Gulf or Levantine Arabic, English, or a mix. Noted detects the language by itself, replies in it and reads it aloud.

Your week at a glance

Home shows what got done this week, how your habits are holding up, your best streak, and a gentle nudge when one is at risk.

FAQ

Questions

Is Noted free?

Yes, Noted is free to download and use. The AI assistant has a fair-use limit of 60 messages a day, which is plenty for planning.

Do I need an account?

No. Continue without an account and your tasks, habits and events are saved only on this phone (chat messages still go to the assistant). Sign in with Apple, Google or email when you want them synced across devices.

Can I type instead of talking?

Yes. Use the "Type to add" bar on Home, or the keyboard button in the chat. Noted understands typed messages exactly like spoken ones.

What's roast mode?

An optional, playful mode where Noted teases you about overdue tasks and missed habits, on Home, in reminders and in chat. It's about your to-do list only, never you, and you can switch it off in Settings anytime.

Are there widgets?

Yes, on iOS 16 and later: Today, Habits (tap a ring to log it, on iOS 17 and later), Quick mic, and Next up for the lock screen. Long-press your home screen, tap +, and search for Noted.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Arabic, including everyday Egyptian, Gulf and Levantine dialects, and a mix of both. In Auto (the default) Noted detects the language for you; you can also pin English or Arabic in the chat or in Settings. The app's menus and buttons are in English for now.

What happens to my voice?

In Auto, your short recording is sent securely to be transcribed (that's how Noted detects Arabic or English) and the audio is not stored. If you pin English or Arabic, your iPhone's own speech recognition is used instead. The assistant itself only receives text: your recent chat and, if Cognitive Memory is on, your tasks, events, lists and habits so it can find and change them.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. Messages are processed by Anthropic's Claude to work out what to do, and Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models. Noted doesn't keep chat history on its server.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later.
